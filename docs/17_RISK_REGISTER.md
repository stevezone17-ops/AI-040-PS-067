## Risk Matrix (`RSK-001` – `RSK-012`)

| Risk ID | Category | Risk Description | Prob. | Impact | Concrete Mitigation Built into Week 1 Design | Contingency Trigger & Action | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-001** | Schema Mismatch | Official sample dataset (when released) uses different CSV column names, single-file enrolments, or different command JSON keys than [`10_INPUT_OUTPUT_CONTRACTS.md`](10_INPUT_OUTPUT_CONTRACTS.md). | High | Med | `ADR-012` isolates all file parsing/formatting in `io/` and `reporting/`. Core domain engines never touch file headers. | Upon sample release, update `io/parsers.py` and `reporting/` adapters first (`< 2 hours` effort). | Mishael |
| **RSK-002** | Policy Divergence | Official expected outputs assume a different provisional interpretation on one of `AS-001`–`AS-033` (e.g., `15`-min buffer on `EC-05` `AS-018`, or calendar vs active final 3 days `AS-015`). | Med | High | Every assumption has an explicit policy-isolation point (`allocation/policies.py`, `invigilation/eligibility.py`, `changes/policies.py`). | Run diff against official expected outputs in `M9`; flip the isolated policy predicate and re-run `pytest`. | Team AI-040 |
| **RSK-003** | Spatial-Temporal Coupling | Aggregate 90% session check passes during scheduling, but halls fail `2h`/`3h` separation, alternate columns, or ground-floor Row 1 seating. | Med | High | Solved architecturally via `ADR-008`: `ALG-06` calls `ALG-08` exact trial hall packing before accepting any candidate session. | Covered by `TC-023`, `TC-030`, `TC-049`. | Steven & Tanisha |
| **RSK-004** | Small-Course Stranding | A `< 10` student course has no compatible cross-department equal-duration partner in its session (or loses its partner during `BR-09` postponement). | Med | High | `ALG-10` pairs small courses into open Slot B halls or co-schedules two small courses; `ALG-15` rejects postponements that orphan a `< 10` partner in `S_orig`. | Verified by `TC-034`–`TC-040` and `TC-077`. | Tanisha |
| **RSK-005** | Blast-Radius Leakage | An incremental change (`BR-09`–`BR-11`) accidentally mutates seats or invigilators in an unaffected session via shared mutable list/dict references. | Med | Critical | Frozen dataclasses + tuple collections + `ALG-19` SHA-256 per-session digest verification before commit (`NFR-03`). | Any digest mismatch raises `InvariantBreachError` immediately during `TC-098`. | Tanisha & Mishael |
| **RSK-006** | Cross-Session Workload Drift | Local re-staffing of `S_dest` on Day 3 assigns an invigilator who already holds `max_weekly_sessions` across fixed Days 4–6, causing a silent `BR-07` breach. | Med | High | `WorkloadTracker.from_fixed_sessions()` loads **all** fixed duties across the entire week/date before staffing affected sessions (`11_INCREMENTAL_RECOMPUTATION.md`). | Audited by `ALG-18` and tested explicitly in `TC-097`. | Mishael |
| **RSK-007** | Non-Determinism Across Environments | Set iteration order in Python varies with `PYTHONHASHSEED`, causing diff failures on evaluator machines. | Low | Critical | Ban raw `set` iteration; require primary-key tie-breakers on every `sorted()` call (`ADR-010`); enforce LF line endings. | Automated CI gate `TC-108` runs subprocesses with randomised `PYTHONHASHSEED`. | Mishael |
| **RSK-008** | Workload Imbalance in Week 3 | Tanisha owns both spatial allocation (`M3`) and incremental changes (`M6`), risking a Week 3 bottleneck. | Med | Med | Quantified in [`12_MODULE_OWNERSHIP.md`](12_MODULE_OWNERSHIP.md) with concrete rebalancing proposal (`R1`: Steven co-implements `ALG-15`; `R2`: Mishael co-implements `ALG-19`). | Confirm rebalancing at Week 1 team review prior to `M6` kickoff. | Team AI-040 |
| **RSK-009** | Parallel Merge Conflicts in Week 2 | All 3 teammates editing shared files simultaneously during `M1`–`M4`. | Med | Low | `M1` locks `domain/entities.py` and `domain/protocols.py` first; `M2` (`scheduling/`), `M3` (`allocation/`), and `M4` (`invigilation/`) reside in disjoint directories! | Enforce feature branches (`feat/m2-scheduling`, `feat/m3-allocation`, `feat/m4-invigilation`) and small PRs. | Steven (W2) |
| **RSK-010** | Viva Silo Vulnerability | A teammate can explain their own package but struggles to live-modify another teammate's module during individual viva (`10` marks + pass gate). | Med | Critical | Mandatory cross-member PR code reviews + [`20_VIVA_PREPARATION.md`](20_VIVA_PREPARATION.md) live-code-change drills across all modules before final submission. | Hold a 60-minute internal mock viva at the end of Week 3 (`M9`). | All 3 Members |
| **RSK-011** | Performance Bottleneck in Trial Packing | Calling `ALG-08` inside `ALG-06` (`120 courses * 20 sessions`) could slow down if `ALG-08` copies student objects repeatedly. | Low | Low | `ALG-08`/`ALG-09` operates purely on integer course headcounts and `special_needs` counts ($\mathcal{O}(H \log H)$), materialising individual `SeatAssignment` objects (`ALG-11`) only **once** per session after course placement finishes! | Benchmark gate `TC-111` verifies `< 5.0 s` (`~0.35 s` expected). | Steven & Tanisha |
| **RSK-012** | Academic Integrity / Code Similarity | Similarity `> 40%` with external repositories or inability to explain design choices. | Low | Critical | Clean-room custom architecture tailored specifically to `PS-067` rules (`BR-01`–`BR-12`), standard-library implementation, and thorough ADR rationale. | Zero external code copying; authentic per-member commits. | All 3 Members |
